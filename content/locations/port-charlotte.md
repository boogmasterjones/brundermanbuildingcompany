      <span class="eyebrow">Local Knowledge</span>
      <h2>Building and Remodeling on Port Charlotte Lots</h2>
      <p>The typical Port Charlotte lot is about 80 feet wide and 125 feet deep. That standard footprint drives everything: where a three-car garage can fit, how deep a lanai and pool can go before hitting the rear setback, and whether an addition is possible without touching the septic drain field. Large areas of Port Charlotte are still on well and septic, while others have county water and sewer; the difference affects both new construction budgets and what can be added to an existing home.</p>
      <p>Canal lots add another layer. Saltwater canals with access to Charlotte Harbor through the Peace and Myakka rivers carry flood-zone designations that set the minimum finished-floor elevation for a new home, and the condition of the seawall matters to the whole project. Freshwater canal lots are generally simpler but still have drainage easements to respect. We evaluate all of this before you settle on a plan.</p>
